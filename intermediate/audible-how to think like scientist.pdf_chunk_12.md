### 1. The Skeptic’s Toolkit: Evaluating Claims
The text outlines a specific methodology for investigating questionable claims to distinguish scientific truth from pseudoscience:
*   **Source Reliability:** Investigate if the claimant has a pattern of making numerous extraordinary or unfounded claims (identifying "cranks").
*   **Independent Verification:** Determine if the claim has been verified by sources outside the claimant's own "belief circle."
*   **Search for Disconfirmation:** Actively seek evidence that *disproves* the claim rather than just seeking confirming evidence (combating **confirmation bias**).
*   **Requirement for Alternatives:** A valid scientific challenge must provide a viable alternative explanation for observed phenomena, rather than simply denying the existing accepted model.
*   **Identification of Bias:** Evaluate whether the conclusion is driven by the data or by the researcher's personal, social, or political ideologies.
