Based on the document segment provided, here are the key insights and facts regarding the **Singapore Cyber Landscape 2022**:

### 1. Ransomware Trends
* **Case Volume:** The number of reported ransomware cases in 2022 remained high at **132 cases**, which is comparable to the 137 cases reported in 2021.
* **Primary Targets:** Small and Medium Enterprises (**SMEs**) in the **Manufacturing and Retail** sectors were the most frequently targeted victims.
* **Prevalent Strains:** Key ransomware strains observed included **LockBit, DeadBolt, and MedusaLocker**.
    * **DeadBolt:** Specifically targets Internet-facing, unprotected Network Attached Storage (NAS) systems.
    * **LockBit 3.0:** Notable for "capability integration," having adopted features from other threats like BlackMatter.
* **Evolution of Tactics:** The landscape is moving toward **Ransomware-as-a-Service (RaaS)** and "cross-platform" capabilities (targeting Windows, Linux, and Mac) to increase efficiency for attackers.

### 2. Phishing Activity
* **Targeting Tactics:** Attackers used phishing emails and SMS messages masquerading as notifications for **incoming deliveries or shipment issues** to exploit user trust.
* **Bank Spoofing:** A significant number of phishing attempts targeted **China-based banks** (e.g., Agricultural Bank of China, Zhongyuan Bank, and China Minsheng Bank) that have little to no presence in the Singapore retail banking scene.
* **Changing Formats:** There was a notable decrease in the average length of reported phishing links, dropping from approximately **44 characters in 2021 to 26 characters in 2022**, likely due to the use of URL shortener services.

### 3. Malware and Infected Infrastructure
* **Infrastructure Decline:** There was a **13% decrease** in the number of locally hosted Command & Control (C&C) servers, falling from 94,000 in 2021 to **81,500 in 2022**. Singapore's global share of infected IP addresses also dropped from 0.84% to 0.34%.
* **Top Malware (C&C Servers):** The most common malware found on locally hosted C&C servers were **Cobalt Strike, Emotet, and Guloader**. 
    * *Note:* Emotet saw a significant resurgence in 2022 due to law enforcement crackdowns in 2021.
* **Top Malware (Botnet Drones):** The most prevalent malware in infected botnet drones were **Gamarue, Nymaim, and Mirai**.
* **Emerging Threats:** **Guloader** is noted as a growing threat, acting as a platform for deploying other malware like Netwire and Agent Tesla.

### 4. Website Defacements
* **Statistical Decrease:** There was a **19% decrease** in the defacement of **'.sg' websites** in 2022 (340 websites) compared to 2021.
* **Target Demographics:** **SMEs** represented the vast majority of victims; notably, **no government websites** were impacted.
* **Vulnerabilities:** 
    * **WordPress** was the most targeted platform; approximately **60%** of defaced WordPress sites were running outdated versions (pre-6.0).
    * **Re-defacements** (websites being defaced multiple times) accounted for nearly **40%** of all observed defacements.
* **Attacker Groups:** Notable activity was driven by groups such as **"Hunter Bajwa"** and **"B3g0k[Kurdish Hacker]"**, whose attacks were often opportunistic and used automated scripts.
