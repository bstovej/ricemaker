### **Part 2: The Psychology and Evolution of Religion**

**Key Insights:**
* **Evolutionary Advantage:** Belief in higher powers or supernatural agents may have provided an evolutionary advantage by fostering social cohesion, altruism, and group cooperation (the "pro-social" function of religion).

    * **Agenticity:** The tendency to attribute intention or "agency" to inanimate objects or natural phenomena (e.g., believing a storm is "angry").
* **Biological/Genetic Basis:** 
    * The text suggests that the capacity for religious belief may be partially heritable and linked to how the brain processes agency and patterns.

* **The "Pro-social" Hypothesis:** Religion acts as a "social glue," helping large groups of unrelated humans cooperate through shared rituals, morals, and the fear of supernatural retribution.
