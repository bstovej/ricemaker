This document reports on the announcement of 360 Group’s enterprise-level AI strategy, presented by founder Zhou Hongyi at the GPT Industry Alliance Founding Conference in Beijing.

Below are the key facts and strategic insights derived from the text.

### **Key Facts**
*   **Major Announcement:** 360 Group has officially launched its **"360 Smart Brain API"** platform, part of a new enterprise-level large-scale model strategy.
*   **Target Industries:** The strategy aims to provide solutions for approximately **20 specific industries**, including security, media, cultural tourism, government affairs, and energy.
*   **Core Principles:** 360’s development of these models is guided by four pillars: **Safety, Credibility, Controllability, and Ease of use.**
*   **Implementation Approach:** The company is adopting a **"small incision, big in depth"** methodology, starting with the "office scene" (improving existing office workflows) before moving to deeper industrial integration.
*   **Event Context:** The announcement took place at the GPT Industry Alliance Founding Conference, co-sponsored by the China Internet Society and the China Academy of Information and Communication Research Institute.

### **Key Strategic Insights**

#### **1. The Shift from "General AI" to "Industrial AI"**
Zhou Hongyi posits that the "blue ocean" market is not in general-purpose consumer AI, but in the enterprise sector. He argues that for AI to drive an industrial revolution, it must move away from being a standalone "spectacle" and instead become a "standard configuration" of digital systems—integrated, low-profile, and functional.

#### **2. Identifying the "Enterprise Gap" in Public Models**
The document highlights a critical weakness in using public large-scale models (like standard GPT) for corporate use. 360 identifies four specific pain points that their strategy intends to solve:
*   **Lack of depth:** Public models lack specialized industry knowledge.
*   **Security risks:** Data privacy concerns when using public clouds.
*   **Unreliability:** The "hallucination" problem (inability to guarantee credible content).
*   **High costs:** The prohibitive expense of training and deploying custom models.

#### **3. Incremental Integration Strategy ("Loose Coupling")**
A significant insight is 360's deployment philosophy. Rather than suggesting that enterprises must overhaul their entire digital infrastructure to adopt AI, 360 advocates for **"loose coupling."** By starting with office applications that integrate seamlessly with existing systems without destroying them, 360 lowers the barrier to entry for traditional enterprises.

#### **4. The Concept of "AI Inclusiveness"**
The strategy emphasizes that AI should not just be a tool for "top-down" decision-making by executives, but a "bottom-up" utility for employees. By solving "pain points and rigid needs" at the worker level, the goal is to achieve "AI inclusiveness" across all levels of an organization.

#### **5. Emphasis on Governance and Ethics**
The inclusion of statements from the China Internet Society and CAICT underscores that the future of the GPT industry in China is heavily tied to **responsible AI.** The consensus among stakeholders is that for AI to be adopted, it must move beyond being "usable" to being "reliable and credible," with a heavy focus on ethical governance and data security.
