Based on the provided document segment from the **Singapore Cyber Landscape 2022**, here are the key insights and facts organized by theme.

### 1. Cybersecurity Ecosystem & Innovation (Foundational Enabler 1)
The document highlights Singapore’s strategy to build a self-sustaining ecosystem through research, development, and industry-government collaboration.

*   **National Cybersecurity R&D (NCR) Programme:** This program aims to improve the trustworthiness of cyber infrastructure and promote collaboration between government agencies (including CSA, MHA, MINDEF, and GovTech) and the private sector.
*   **CyberCall Initiative:** 
    *   A CSA-led industry call for innovation designed to connect cybersecurity companies with large, trusted end-users (such as owners of critical infrastructure).
    *   **Fact:** Since its inception, CyberCall has supported **22 cybersecurity companies** and awarded a total of **S$10 million** across various projects.
*   **Case Study: Scantist:** 
    *   A successful spin-off from the Nanyang Technological University (NTU) cybersecurity laboratory.
    *   Scantist benefited from the NCR Programme and CSA's "Call for Innovation," using these resources to expand its team and commercialize its "Software Composition Analysis" product.

### 2. Talent Development & Workforce (Foundational Enabler 2)
Singapore is actively investing in a "pipeline" of talent ranging from youth to senior leadership.

*   **SG Cyber Olympians:** 
    *   A program designed to train Singaporean youths for international competitions (e.g., DEFCON, Cyber SEA Games).
    *   **Fact:** In 2022, 47 youths were identified for the program, with seven top performers selected to represent Singapore. The Singapore team notably won **second place** at the Cyber SEA Games 2022 in Bangkok.
*   **Cybersecurity Strategic Leadership Programme (CSLP):** 
    *   A partnership between **CSA and Singapore Management University (SMU)**. 
    *   It is an executive MBA-style program for senior leaders in Critical Information Infrastructure (CII) sectors (e.g., Energy, Finance, Healthcare).
    *   **Fact:** The inaugural session (Sept 2022 – Jan 2023) included **21 local senior leaders**.
*   **Smart Nation Scholarship:** 
    *   A joint initiative by **CSA, GovTech, and IMDA** to recruit talent into the public service.
    *   **Fact:** In 2022, **17 scholarships** were awarded, covering domains such as Cybersecurity, Data Science & AI, and Tech Policy & Governance.

### 3. International Cooperation & Norms
The document emphasizes Singapore’s role in international cybersecurity diplomacy and the establishment of global norms.

*   **ASCCE Webinar Series:** The ASEAN-Singapore Cybersecurity Centre of Excellence (ASCCE) launched a webinar series during the COVID-19 pandemic to maintain capacity-building momentum for ASEAN Member States (AMS).
*   **Norms Implementation Checklist Workshop:** 
    *   A joint initiative with the **UNODA** (United Nations Office for Disarmament Affairs).
    *   The focus is on implementing the "Framework for Responsible State Behaviour in Cyberspace."
    *   Workshops held in 2022 (one virtual, one at UN Headquarters) aimed to develop checklists for protecting critical infrastructure and reporting ICT vulnerabilities.

### 4. Incident Response & Readiness
*   **Exercise Cyber Knights 2021 (XCK21):** Conducted between November 2021 and January 2022, this exercise was specifically designed to sharpen the skills of Singapore’s cyber responders.
