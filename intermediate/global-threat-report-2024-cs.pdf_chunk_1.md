Based on the provided document segment from the **CrowdStrike 2024 Global Threat Report**, here are the key insights and facts categorized by theme:

### 1. High-Level Threat Trends
* **The Shift to "Stealth" and "Speed":** The primary theme of 2023 was stealth. Adversaries are increasingly using "interactive intrusions" (hands-on-keyboard) and legitimate tools to mimic normal user behavior, making them harder to detect.
* **The Rise of AI:** Generative AI is lowering the barrier to entry for low-skilled attackers, allowing them to launch more sophisticated, state-of-the-art attacks.
* **Reduction in "Breakout Time":** The time it takes for an attacker to move laterally from an initial breach to other parts of a network is shrinking. In 2023, the average breakout time decreased from **84 minutes to 62 minutes**.
* **Malware-Free Attacks:** There is a significant trend toward malware-free activity. In 2023, **75% of detections were malware-free**, an increase from 71% in 2022.

### 2. Key Statistical Insights
* **Cloud Vulnerability:** Cloud environment intrusions increased by **75%** year-over me. Notably, **84%** of cloud-conscious intrusions were attributed to eCrime.
* **eCrime Growth:** There was a **76% increase** in the number of victims named on Big Game Hunting (BGH) dedicated leak sites between 2022 and 2023.
* **Access Brokers:** The number of advertised access opportunities provided by brokers increased by **20%**, acting as a primary engine for eCrime.
* **Identity Attacks:** There was a massive **583% increase in Kerberoasting attacks** in 2023.
* **Adversary Expansion:** CrowdStrike tracked **34 new adversaries** in 2023, bringing the total number of tracked actors to **232**.

### 3. Adversary Profiles & Motivations
* **eCrime (Spider):** The most pervasive threat. Focused on data-theft extortion and "Big Game Hunting."
* **Nation-State Actors:**
    * **China (Panda):** Operating at an unmatched pace, focusing on surveillance, strategic intelligence, and intellectual property theft.
    * **Russia (Bear):** Focused on disruption and information operations, particularly in support of the Russia-Ukraine war (e.g., FANCY BEAR and COZY BEAR).
    * **North Korea (Chollima):** Maintaining a high tempo focused on financial gain via cryptocurrency theft and intelligence collection.
    * **Iran (Kitten):** Observed pivoting operations in alignment with the Israel-Hamas conflict.
* **Scattered Spider:** Identified as a highly sophisticated social engineering specialist using techniques like **smishing (SMS phishing)**, **vishing (voice phishing)**, and **SIM swapping** to target IT and security professionals.

### 4. Evolving Attack Vectors
* **Identity-Based Attacks:** Adversaries are moving away from traditional malware toward stealing:
    * Account credentials and API keys.
    * Session cookies and tokens.
    * One-Time Passwords (OTPs) via MFA interception.
    * Kerberos tickets.
* **Exploitation of Trust:** Increasing use of supply chain attacks and the exploitation of "trusted" software and third-party relationships to maximize the Return on Investment (ROI) of an attack.
* **Cloud-Conscious Attacks:** As businesses move to the cloud, attackers are specifically developing capabilities to exploit cloud-unique features and gaps in cloud protection.
