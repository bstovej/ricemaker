This document segment is a standard legal disclosure and disclaimer from **Goldman Sachs Global Investment Research (Issue 146)**. It outlines the risks associated with investing, the protocols for information dissemination, and strict limitations on how the provided data may be used.

The following are the key insights and facts categorized by theme:

### 1. Investment Risk Disclosures
*   **Market Volatility:** The value of investments and the income derived from them are subject to fluctuation.
*   **No Guarantees:** Past performance does not guarantee future results, and there is a documented risk of losing the original invested capital.
*   **Currency Risk:** Fluctuations in exchange rates can negatively impact the value or income of certain investments.
*   **Derivative Risks:** Transactions involving futures, options, and other derivatives carry "substantial risk" and are not suitable for all investors.
*   **Cost Awareness:** Investors are cautioned that transaction costs (particularly for complex strategies like spreads) can be significant.

### 2. Information Parity and Service Levels
*   **Standardized Distribution:** All research reports are released to all eligible clients **simultaneously** via electronic publication on Goldman Sachs' internal websites.
*   **No Preferential Information:** A critical rule is stated: **No changes to an analyst’s fundamental views** (e.g., changes in ratings, price targets, or earnings estimates) will be communicated to any specific client before the information is officially published in a research report available to all entitled clients.
*   **Variable Service Tiers:** While information parity is maintained regarding research views, the *level of service* may vary. This includes differences in communication frequency, data feed types (e.g., electronic data feeds), and the scope of the client relationship.

### 3. Intellectual Property and Usage Restrictions
This section contains highly specific legal prohibitions regarding the use of the research:
*   **Permitted Use:** Users are only permitted to store, display, analyze, modify, reformat, and print the information for **their own personal/internal use.**
*   **Prohibited Commercial Use:** Users may not resell, reverse engineer, or use the data to create indices, derivative works, or any commercial products/offerings without express written consent.
*   **Prohibited Redistribution:** The information cannot be published or transmitted to any third party without written consent.
*   **AI and Machine Learning Restriction (Critical Clause):** The document explicitly prohibits using the research content to **train, fine-tune, or provide inputs/prompts to machine learning or artificial intelligence (AI) systems.**
