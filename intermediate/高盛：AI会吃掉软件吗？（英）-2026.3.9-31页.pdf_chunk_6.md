### 2. Analytical Methodologies
The document details the proprietary frameworks Goldman Sachs uses to evaluate stocks:

**GS Factor Profile**
This tool compares a stock to its sector peers and the broader market using four attributes:
*   **Growth:** Based on forward-looking sales, EBITDA, and EPS growth.
*   **Financial Returns:** Based on forward-looking ROE, ROCE, and CROCI.
*   **Multiple (Valuation):** Based on forward-looking P/E, P/B, P/D, EV/EBITDA, and other metrics.
*   **Integrated:** A composite score representing the average of Growth, Financial Returns, and a reversed Multiple percentile (100% - Multiple percentile).

**M&A Rank**
Goldman Sachs assigns a 1–3 score to indicate the probability of a company becoming an acquisition target:
*   **Rank 1:** High probability (30%–50%).
*   **Rank 2:** Medium probability (15%–30%).
*   **Rank 3:** Low probability (0%–15%).
*   *Note:* For Ranks 1 and 2, the M&A component is incorporated into the analyst's price target.
