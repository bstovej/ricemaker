#### **Technical "Tips and Tricks" for Prompting**
*   **Syntax Control:** 
    *   Use **curly brackets `{}`** to define parameters or instructions.
    *   Use a **colon `:`** to indicate text that should be processed or rephrased.
    *   Use **quotes `""`** to clarify specific terminology.
*   **Operational Commands:**
    *   **"Reset the chat"**: Clears the model's memory of previous training within a session.
    *   **"Continue"**: Solves the issue of truncated responses.
    *   **"Let’s play a game!"**: A strategic way to bypass certain AI conversational restrictions or limitations.
    *   **"Are you sure?"**: A technique to force the model to double-check its mathematical or factual accuracy.
*   **Output Optimization:**
    *   **Temperature 0.2**: Recommended for project management tasks to ensure the AI remains focused and avoids "hallucinations" or excessive creativity.
    *   **Avoidance List**: To reduce "AI-detection," the text advises against using common AI-generated "clichés" such as *spearheaded, fostered, delve, prowess,* and *adept*.
    *   **Learning Levels**: The AI can be instructed to explain complex subjects at specific levels (e.g., Grade 5, University, or Professor).
