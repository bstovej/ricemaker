This document outlines two distinct and highly effective study methodologies: **The Leitner System** (a method for spaced repetition) and **The Feynman Technique** (a method for deep comprehension).

Here are the key insights and facts from the text:

### 1. The Leitner System (Spencially Focused on Retention)
The primary goal of this system is to optimize memory retention by focusing energy on difficult material while reducing the frequency of review for mastered content.

*   **Core Mechanism:** It uses a system of progressive "review boxes" (physical or digital) to organize flashcards.
*   **The Review Schedule:** The frequency of review is determined by the box level:
    *   **Box 1:** Daily review.
    *   **Box 2:** Weekly review.
    *   **Box 3:** Monthly review.
*   **The Process of Advancement:** As you review, if you can answer a card correctly and without hesitation, it is moved to a subsequent box. This ensures that "easy" information is reviewed less often, freeing up time for "challenging" information.
*   **Recommended Tools:** The text suggests using flashcard applications like **Anki** or **Quizlet**.

### 2. The Feynman Technique (Focused on Comprehension)
Named after physicist Richard Feynman, this technique is based on the principle of **simplification**. It is designed to move a learner from passive reading to active mastery.

*   **The Fundamental Principle:** If you cannot explain a concept simply (as if to a child), you do not fully understand it.
*   **The Iterative Process:**
    1.  **Choose and Teach:** Select a topic and attempt to explain it using simple language, strictly avoiding technical jargon.
    2.  **Identify Gaps:** The "teaching" phase serves as a diagnostic tool. When you struggle to explain a point or find yourself relying on jargon, you have identified a "gap" in your knowledge.
    3.  **Relearn and Refine:** Return to the source material to study the specific areas where you struggled. 
    4.  **Simplify Further:** Use analogies and everyday examples to refine the explanation until the concept is clear, concise, and easy to communicate.
*   **Key Benefits:** It promotes **active learning**, identifies specific weaknesses in understanding, and improves the ability to communicate complex ideas effectively.
