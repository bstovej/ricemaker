This document is a formal proposal for a **Professional Certificate Course in "AI/Machine Learning for Cybersecurity"** at the **NUS School of Computing**. 

The course is designed to bridge the gap between artificial intelligence and cybersecurity, offering a multidisciplinary curriculum that spans technical implementation, strategic management, and entrepreneurial venture creation.

### 1. Course Overview & Structure
*   **Total Duration:** 49 hours.
*   **Core Objective:** To equip professionals with the ability to design, implement, and manage AI-powered cybersecurity solutions, while also understanding the business and ethical implications.
*   **The Three-Module Framework:**
    *   **Module 1 (Fundamentals):** Foundations of AI/ML (Supervised, Unsupervised, Deep Learning, Generative AI) and their specific applications in threat detection, malware analysis, and phishing prevention.
    *   **Module 2 (Strategic Management):** Focuses on the "big picture"—MLOps, risk management, AI ethics, governance, regulatory compliance, and managing human capital.
    *   **Module 3 (Elective Tracks):** Participants choose one of two specialized directions:
        *   **Module 3A (Technical Track):** Intensive lab sessions involving hands-on programming, penetration testing using ML, deepfake recognition, and securing data via federated learning.
        *   **Module 3B (Entrepreneurial Track):** Focuses on "Technopreneurship"—market research, business models (B2B/B2C), venture financing (Seed to Series C), and pitching to investors.

### 2. Key Learning Domains
*   **Technical Depth:** The course covers cutting-edge topics including **Generative AI** (for both attack simulation and defense), **Deepfakes**, **Adversarial Attacks**, and **Federated Learning**.
*   **Operational Management:** Beyond coding, the course emphasizes **MLOps** (Machine Learning Operations), ensuring that AI models are not just built but also deployed, monitored, and maintained in production environments.
*   **Business & Innovation:** The curriculum includes a "Go-to-Market" strategy, covering product-market fit, pricing strategies, and the lifecycle of a cybersecurity startup (from Proof of Concept to Launch).

### 3. Target Audience
The course is uniquely positioned to serve five distinct professional groups:
1.  **Cybersecurity Professionals** looking to add AI/ML skills.
2.  **IT Professionals** seeking to bridge the gap into cybersecurity.
3.  **Cybersecurity Managers** requiring a non-technical understanding of AI integration.
4.  **Consultants/Advisors** in the AI and security space.
5.  **Government Officers/Regulators** responsible for cybersecurity policy and AI governance.

### 4. Notable Facts & Highlights
*   **Real-World Context:** The curriculum incorporates high-profile industry case studies, including **Netflix** (anomaly detection), **Google** (incident response), **Palo Alto Networks** (threat intelligence), and **PayPal** (fraud prevention).
*   **Hands-on Tools:** The technical track utilizes industry-standard tools such as **Python, TensorFlow, Keras, Scikit-learn, Kali Linux,** and **Docker/Kubernetes**.
*   **Expert Faculty:** The course is led by **Zaid Hamzah**, an AI patent holder and expert in AI governance, law, and cybersecurity, alongside a team of specialized trainers in data science and technical labs.

### Summary Insight
This is not a standard "coding bootcamp." It is a **comprehensive professional development program** that treats AI in cybersecurity as a holistic ecosystem involving **technology** (how to build it), **strategy** (how to govern it), and **economy** (how to commercialize it).
