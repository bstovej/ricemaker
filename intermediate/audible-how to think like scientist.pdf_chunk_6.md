### 1. The Logic of Scientific Inquiry
*   **The Null Hypothesis and Uncertainty:** Science operates on the principle of testing against a "null hypothesis." In the context of the text, one does not prove a phenomenon exists; rather, one fails to disprove the idea that it *doesn't* exist.
*   **The Burden of Proof:** The "burden of proof" lies with the person making the claim. In scientific terms, the claimant must provide evidence to move away from the null hypothesis; it is not the responsibility of the skeptic to disprove the claim.

The text distinguishes between legitimate scientific inquiry and pseudoscience through several lenses:

### 5. Summary of Scientific Methodology
*   **Skepticism is foundational:** Skepticism is not the denial of truth, but the demand for sufficient evidence before accepting a claim.
*   **Evidence-Based:** Scientific progress relies on the ability to replicate findings and the rigorous application of the scientific method to test the validity of observations.
*   **Self-Correction:** Science is a process of constant refinement where theories are discarded or updated as new, contradictory evidence emerges.
