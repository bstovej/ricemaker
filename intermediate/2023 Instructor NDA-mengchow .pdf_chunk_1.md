This document is a comprehensive **Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (MNDA)**. Its purpose is to establish the rules and protections governing the exchange of confidential, proprietary, and sensitive information between two parties—in this case, the International Information System Security Certification Consortium, Inc. (**ISC2**) and an individual (Averitus Pte Ltd).

Here are the key insights and facts, organized by topic:

---

### 🔑 Key Insights and Facts

#### 📜 **Parties and Purpose**
*   **Parties:** International Information System Security Certification Consortium, Inc. (ISC2) and the individual/entity representing Averitus Pte Ltd.
*   **Goal:** To allow the parties to share non-public, confidential, or proprietary information ("Confidential Information") for a defined purpose ("the Purpose") related to present or future business arrangements.

#### 💡 **Scope of Confidential Information (What is Protected)**
The definition of Confidential Information is extremely broad, covering almost any non-public data. It includes, but is not limited to:
*   **General Business Information (1.a):** Finances, customer/member lists, training materials, examination data, supplier information, organizational structure, and sales/marketing strategies.
*   **Highly Sensitive Information (1.b):** This is the highest level of protection and includes:
    *   Nonpublic Personal Information (names, addresses, SSNs, tax IDs, account numbers, etc.) for customers, members, and employees.
    *   Computer security details (encryption keys, software source code, security logs, PINs/passwords).
*   **Intellectual Property (1.c & 1.d):** Trade secrets, know-how, unpatented inventions, ideas, source code, object code, and schematics.
*   **Derivations (1.f):** Crucially, it protects "Notes," which are reports, analyses, summaries, or materials *prepared by* the Recipient based on the confidential data, even if those Notes contain nothing original.

#### 🚫 **Exclusions (What is NOT Protected)**
Information does not qualify as confidential if it meets any of the following criteria:
1.  It is already generally public knowledge (not due to a breach).
2.  It becomes available from a third party who was not legally restricted from sharing it.
3.  The Recipient already possessed the information (with documentary proof) before the disclosure.
4.  The Recipient independently developed the information (with documentary proof) without using the Disclosing Party's confidential data.

#### 🛡️ **Recipient Obligations (The Rules of Conduct)**
The Recipient (the party receiving the information) must:
*   **Standard of Care:** Protect the information with at least the same degree of care the Recipient uses to protect its own confidential information, but no less than "commercially reasonable care."
*   **Limited Use:** Use the information *only* for the agreed-upon "Purpose" and nothing else (e.g., specifically prohibits reverse engineering or decompiling proprietary services).
*   **Need-to-Know Basis:** Not disclose the information, except to its necessary Representatives (employees, advisors) who are also bound by strict confidentiality duties.
*   **Acknowledge Confidentiality:** Must not disclose that the information was made available or that discussions are even taking place (Section 4).

#### 🚨 **Survival, Termination, and Remedies**
*   **Survival Period:** While the parties can terminate the agreement at any time, the confidentiality obligations for **Highly Sensitive Information** and information that constitutes a **trade secret** survive the termination and continue indefinitely, unless the information loses its trade secret status through no fault of the Recipient.
*   **General Survival Period:** All other obligations survive for a period of **two (2) years** from the date of termination.
*   **Return/Destruction:** Upon request, the Recipient must promptly return or destroy all copies and provide written certification of destruction.
*   **Legal Order:** If disclosure is legally required (e.g., by a subpoena), the Recipient must attempt to notify the Disclosing Party immediately so the Disclosing Party can seek a protective order. The Recipient may only disclose the minimum amount required by law.
*   **Remedies:** The parties agree that money damages are insufficient, and therefore, the non-breaching party is entitled to seek **specific performance and injunctive relief**.

#### 🌍 **Governing Law and Logistics**
*   **Governing Law:** The agreement is governed by the internal laws of the **Commonwealth of Virginia**.
*   **Jurisdiction:** Any legal action must be filed in the federal or state courts located in **Alexandria, Virginia**.
*   **Warranties & Liability:** Neither party warrants the accuracy or completeness of the information provided. The Disclosing Party is not liable for errors or omissions.
*   **No Transfer of Rights:** The disclosure of information does not grant any license, option, or ownership rights to the Recipient.
