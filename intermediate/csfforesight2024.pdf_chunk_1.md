This document is the **15th Anniversary Issue (2024) of *Foresight***, a publication by the **Centre for Strategic Futures (CSF)**, part of the Singapore Public Service. It commemorates 15 years of strategic foresight work in Singapore.

Below are the key insights and facts categorized by theme:

### 1. Institutional Overview: The Centre for Strategic Futures (CSF)
*   **Purpose:** The CSF is tasked with developing insights into emerging trends and translating them into actionable strategies to help the Singapore Government navigate an unpredictable world.
*   **History & Evolution:** 
    *   Established in **2009** within the Public Service Division.
    *   Moved to the **Strategy Group in the Prime Minister’s Office in 2015**.
    *   Has evolved from an "amorphous new entity" to a central pillar of the Singapore Public Service’s forward-looking capability.
*   **Capability Building:** The Centre manages the **"FutureCraft"** course series at the Civil Service College and has developed a specific **foresight competencies framework** to grow talent within the government.

### 2. Strategic Context & Global Challenges
The document (through forewords by Prime Minister Lawrence Wong and Head of Civil Service Leo Yip) identifies several critical "fronts of change" that Singapore must navigate:
*   **Geopolitics:** Intensifying global tensions, conflicts, wars, and economic fragmentation.
*   **Technology:** The rapid rise of **Generative AI** and the "Bio meets Byte" intersection.
*   **Environment & Demographics:** The urgent climate crisis and shifting demographic trends (e.g., aging populations).
*   **Singapore’s Mandate:** As a small, open city-state, Singapore relies on **"agility and nimbleness"** and long-term planning (e.g., Tuas Mega Port and Changi Terminal 5) to maintain its status as a global hub.

### 3. Key Achievements & Activities (2021–2023)
*   **Major Events:** 
    *   *Futures Conversation 2021:* Focused on "50 Shades of Power" (changing power dynamics).
    *   *Futures Conversation 2023:* Focused on "Ways of Seeing: Narratives amidst Uncertainty."
*   **Research Deep Dives:** The Centre has conducted high-level research into topics including interest rate volatility, social atomization, mortality (Mortality 4.0), and the impact of technology on society.
*   **Global Engagement:** The CSF maintains a high-level international network, having hosted experts from Columbia University, the US Department of Defense, the Oxford Martin School, and the Sony Group Corporation.

### 4. Notable Legacy: Dick O’Neill
A significant portion of the document serves as a tribute to the late **Richard (Dick) O’Neill**, a Distinguished Fellow:
*   He was instrumental in the creation of the **"Island Forum"** for the Ministry of Defence (MINDEF).
*   He helped establish the **Risk Assessment and Horizon Scanning (RAHS)** programme.
*   He is credited with connecting the Singapore government to global think tanks (e.g., RAND Corporation, Atlantic Council) and fostering a culture of "moonshot dreams" and experimentation within the CSF.

### 5. The "15 by 15" Anniversary Initiative
To celebrate 15 years, the Centre launched a series of short essays titled **"Another ________ is Possible."** 
*   The goal is to challenge existing assumptions and reimagine the future.
*   The featured excerpt, *"Another Language is Possible,"* argues for a new "language of care" to complement current models of efficiency and competition, specifically to address climate change and mental wellness.
