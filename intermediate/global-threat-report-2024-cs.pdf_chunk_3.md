This document is an excerpt from the **CrowdStrike 2024 Global Threat Report**. It provides an analysis of cybersecurity threats arising from the Israel-Hamas conflict, the evolving role of Generative AI in cyberattacks, and the risks associated with the massive wave of global elections in 2024.

Below are the key insights and facts categorized by theme:

### 1. The Israel-Hamas Conflict & Hacktivism
*   **Scale of Activity:** Pro-Palestine hacktivism significantly outpaced pro-Israel activity following the October 7, 2023, attacks.
*   **Primary Targets:** Hacktivists targeted Israeli **critical infrastructure**, including energy distribution, water pumps, and water treatment plants. A major focus was the disruption of **aerial projectile warning systems** (to prevent/falsify rocket alerts).
*   **Globalized Impact:** Hacktivist operations extended beyond the conflict zone:
    *   **USA:** A Yemeni group (Team R70) targeted a U.S. airport; an Indonesian group (INFINITE INSIGHT) allegedly leaked the PII of **790,000 U.S. doctors**.
    *   **UK:** A South Asian group (Team Insane Pakistan) targeted a British military website.
*   **Operational Origin:** Evidence suggests some actors, such as "CruelAlchemy," may operate from locations outside of the Gaza Strip.

### 2. Iranian State-Nexus Cyber Operations
*   **Strategy of Proxies:** While CrowdStrike observed no direct operational support from Iran to Hamas's cyber units, Iranian state-linked actors (MOIS and IRGC) used **"inauthentic personas"** and hacktivist proxies to conduct disruptive operations.
*   **Key Tactics:** These groups used "hack-and-leak" operations, data wiping, and the creation of "faktivist" personas to influence public perception and simulate the disruption of critical services.
*   **Notable Actors & Incidents:**
    *   **Banished Kitten (MOIS-linked):** Deployed the **BiBiWiper** malware against Israeli companies.
    *   **Cyber Av3ngers (IRGC-linked):** Compromised and defaced Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs) in Israel and the U.S. (specifically water treatment facilities).
    *   **Vengeful Kitten (Moses Staff):** Claimed data-wiping activity against Israeli Industrial Control Systems (ICS).
    *   **Haywire Kitten:** Linked to IRGC contractors; targeted CCTV systems at U.S. airports.

### 3. The Impact of Generative AI on Cyber Threats
*   **Democratization of Cybercrime:** Generative AI has lowered the "entry barrier," allowing less sophisticated actors to execute more complex operations.
*   **Two Primary Use Cases:**
    1.  **Malicious Computer Network Operations (CNO):** Using LLMs to write malicious code, scripts, or tools.
        *   *Example:* **INDRIK SPIDER** used ChatGPT to learn how to navigate Azure and exfiltrate credentials.
        *   *Example:* **SCATTERED SPIDER** used LLMs (resembling Llama 2 output) to generate PowerShell scripts.
    2.  **Information Operations (IO) & Social Engineering:** Creating highly deceptive "deepfakes" (audio, video, images) and automated propaganda.
        *   *Example:* A Chinese campaign used **diffusion-model-generated images** to drive engagement on social media.

### 4. 2024 Global Election Risks
*   **Unprecedented Scale:** In 2024, over **42% of the global population** (from 55 countries) will participate in elections, including 7 of the 10 most populous nations (e.g., U.S., India, Indonesia, Mexico).
*   **Three Tiers of Election Interference:**
    1.  **Direct:** Intrusions into hardware/software used to count or transmit votes.
    2.  **Indirect:** DDoS attacks on government websites, hacking and leaking voter registration data, or defacing official sites.
    3.  **Information Operations (Most Common):** Using disinformation to undermine public confidence in election integrity or to polarize the electorate.
*   **Geopolitical Intent:**
    *   **Russia/Iran:** Likely to target the U.S. and EU to exploit political polarization.
    *   **China:** Likely to target its regional sphere of influence (e.g., Taiwan, Indonesia, South Korea).
    *   **India/Pakistan:** Likely to target each other’s elections due to existing regional tensions.
