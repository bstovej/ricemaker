Based on the provided document segment from the **Global Cybersecurity Outlook Report 2025**, here are the key insights and facts categorized by theme:

### 1. The AI Paradox: High Impact, Low Readiness
A significant gap exists between the perceived importance of AI and the ability to secure it.
* **The Impact:** **66%** of organizations believe AI and machine learning will have the greatest impact on cybersecurity over the next 12 months.
* **The Vulnerability:** Despite its impact, **63%** of organizations currently lack the processes necessary to assess the security of AI tools.

### 2. Geopolitical Influence on Strategy
Geopolitics is no longer just a background factor; it is actively reshaping corporate cybersecurity and operational strategies.
* **Strategic Shifts:** **59%** of organizations report that geopolitical tensions have influenced their cybersecurity strategy.
* **Operational Consequences:** These tensions are driving companies to:
    * Modify insurance policies.
    * Change or replace vendors.
    * Stop conducting business/operations in certain countries.
    * Alter trading and operating policies.

### 3. Primary Drivers of Complexity
The report identifies several overlapping layers of complexity that organizations face:
* **Top Challenges:** 
    * **Supply Chain Interdependencies (26%):** Identified as the greatest barrier to cyber resilience.
    * **Increasing Sophistication of Cybercrime (22%):** A major source of concern.
    * **Geopolitical Uncertainty (20%):** A significant driver of complexity.
    * **Rapid Adoption of Emerging Tech (12%):** Adds new layers of risk.
* **Secondary Challenges:** Include the cyber skills gap (7%), expanding/unharmonized regulatory requirements (6%), and IT–OT convergence (6%).

### 4. The Growing "Resilience Gap"
There is a widening disparity in cybersecurity preparedness based on organizational size.
* **Large Organizations:** Showing steady progress toward achieving cyber resilience.
* **Small Organizations:** Significantly struggling. In 2025, **35% of small organizations** believe their cyber-resilience is inadequate—a massive increase from only **7% in 2022**.
