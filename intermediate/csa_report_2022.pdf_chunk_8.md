Based on the document provided (the **Singapore Cyber Landscape 2022** report), here are the key insights and facts organized by strategic area:

### 1. Counter-Ransomware Strategy
Singapore has moved from treating ransomware as a "sporadic criminal activity" to a significant national security threat that requires a coordinated, cross-domain approach.

*   **The Counter-Ransomware Task Force (CRTF):** Commissioned to create a blueprint for government agencies to strengthen Singapore's posture, thwart criminal groups, and support victims.
*   **The Four Pillars of Counter-Ransomware:**
    1.  **Strengthen Defenses:** Protecting high-risk targets like Critical Information Infrastructure (CII) and businesses.
    2.  **Disrupt the Business Model:** Reducing the financial payoff for attackers.
    3.  **Support Recovery:** Ensuring victims do not feel pressured to pay ransoms, which prevents the fueling of the ransomware industry.
    4.  **International Cooperation:** Working with global partners to take down the criminal industry.
*   **Global Leadership:** Singapore played a leading role in the **Cyber Resilience Initiative (CRI)**, a plurilateral effort involving 39+ countries. Singapore also leveraged international platforms like the Financial Action Task Force (FATF) and the UN Office on Drugs and Crime (UNODC) to combat the global ransomware trade.

### 2. Key Cybersecurity Initiatives
The document highlights three major operational programs managed by the Cyber Security Agency (CSA):

*   **Asset Based Cyber Defence (ABCD):**
    *   **Goal:** To protect SMEs and individuals from malware, data theft, and insider threats.
    *   **Model:** Uses a **Security-as-a-Service (SecaaS)** model via Managed Security Service Providers (MSSPs) providing 24/7 monitoring.
    *   **Impact:** As of May 2022, over **500 SMEs** were onboarded, and the program successfully detected and defended against nearly **4,000 cyber threats**.
*   **Cybersecurity Services Regulation Office (CSRO):**
    *   **Launched:** April 11, 2022.
    *   **Purpose:** To license providers of high-access services (like penetration testing and managed SOC monitoring) to protect consumer interests and address "information asymmetry."
    *   **Status:** As of Dec 31, 2022, the office received ~730 applications and approved approximately **400 licenses**.
*   **National Cyber Threat Monitoring Centre (NCTMC) & Proactive Threat Watch:**
    *   **Function:** Generates threat reports for stakeholders.
    *   **2022 Achievement:** In collaboration with SingCERT, the initiative informed companies of over **5,000 leaked credentials** and identified over **50 phishing websites**.

### 3. International Engagement & Diplomacy
Singapore uses high-level forums to promote cyber resilience and international cooperation.

*   **7th Singapore International Cyber Week (SICW):** A major platform for dialogue between government, civil society, and industry. The 2022 event saw significant global participation, including ministerial officials from the US, UK, Australia, and ASEAN members, as well as new attendees from countries like Japan, Canada, and the Netherlands.
*   **7th ASEAN Ministerial Conference on Cybersecurity (AMCC):** Focuses on capacity building, cyber diplomacy, and bilateral/international engagements to foster a stable and interoperable cyberspace.
