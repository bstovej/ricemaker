### 3. Civil Procedure and Due Process
The document outlines the mechanics of how the law is applied through the lens of "Due Process."

* **Substantive vs. Procedural Law:** 
    * **Substantive Law** defines rights and duties (e.g., what constitutes a crime).
    * **Procedural Law** defines the "rules of the game"—how those rights are enforced in court (e.g., how a trial is conducted).
* **The Concept of Due Process:** 
    * Rooted in the 5th and 14th Amendments, Due Process requires that the government follow fair procedures before depriving a person of "life, liberty, or property."
    * **The "Goldberg" Standard:** The text references *Goldberg v. Kelly*, noting that "property" in a legal sense can include government benefits (like welfare), and the state cannot terminate them without a fair hearing.
