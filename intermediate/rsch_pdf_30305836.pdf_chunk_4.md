This document, a **Citi GPS Global Perspectives & Solutions report (January 2025)**, explores the transformative impact of Generative AI (GenAI) and **Agentic AI** across the financial services landscape.

The central theme is the shift from "passive" AI (which generates content) to "agentic" AI (which can execute tasks, make decisions, and manage complex workflows autonomously).

### 1. Security & Fraud Prevention: The "AI Arms Race"
The document highlights an emerging "arms race" between attackers (some with state-level resources) and defenders.
*   **The Threat:** GenAI-based deepfakes (audio and video) are becoming more believable and scalable, making it easier to impersonate high-net-worth individuals, politicians, and bank employees.
*   **The Vulnerability:** A critical fact noted is that **9 out of 10 KYC (Know Your Customer) systems utilizing liveness detection can currently be bypassed** by deepfakes.
*   **The Defense:** Financial institutions are moving toward real-time analysis of audio/video streams. 
    *   *Example:* **Reality Defender** was used by a major bank to identify approximately 37 potentially manipulated calls per week, demonstrating the ability to detect threats without disrupting conversation flow.

### 2. Operational Efficiency: Onboarding, KYC, and Credit
Agentic AI is transforming back-office, resource-intensive processes into automated workflows.
*   **Onboarding & KYC:** Beyond mere verification, Agentic AI analyzes "contextual signals" (e.g., how a device is held) to prevent fraud. 
    *   *Example:* **Mastercard**, in collaboration with **Databricks**, launched GenAI assistants using Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) to automate product onboarding.
*   **Credit & Loan Workflows:** The document highlights the move toward **Small Language Models (SLMs)**. Unlike massive models, SLMs are specialized for specific tasks (e.g., risk assessment, compliance).
    *   *Example:* **Arcee AI** uses a "network" of specialized SLMs to decompose complex tasks like credit memo drafting into automated steps (data collection $\rightarrow$ financial analysis $\rightarrow$ risk assessment $\rightarrow$ drafting).

### 3. Agentic Contracts & Private Markets
The technology is shifting legal agreements from static "PDF documents" to "programmable, enforceable" entities.
*   **Smart Legal Contracts (SLCs):** Agentic AI can manage the **post-signature phase** of a contract, such as monitoring compliance, automating payments, and managing regulatory changes.
*   **Real-world Application:** In maritime private credit, agentic contracts can monitor vessel locations to ensure they do not enter sanctioned waters and automatically adjust loan schedules based on interest rate changes.

### 4. Corporate Treasury & Wealth Management
*   **Treasury Optimization:** "Agentic treasurers" use AI to automate cash forecasting, reconciliation, and the reallocation of funds to highest-yielding opportunities.
    *   *Key Workflow:* The process involves enriching data, identifying patterns (e.g., customer payment delays), forecasting, and validating results.
*   **Wealth Management Augmentation:** AI is not replacing advisors but augmenting them.
    *   *Example:* **Pictet Group** uses "One.Chat," an AI platform that saves employees an estimated **1.5–2 hours per week**. It allows relationship managers to filter massive investment universes using natural language (e.g., "find bonds with X maturity and Y yield").
*   **Hyper-Personalization:** Agentic AI enables "hyper-personalization" by analyzing real-time data to tailor marketing, products, and investment recommendations to individual behaviors.

### 5. Insurance: Revolutionizing Claims
*   **Speed of Resolution:** Agentic AI can reduce insurance claim resolution times from **months or weeks to just days**.
*   **Automation Capabilities:** AI agents can handle initial reporting, triage claims by severity, analyze photos/videos for damage assessment, and even trigger instant payouts for straightforward cases.

### Summary of Key Technological Trends
| Technology | Primary Function in Finance |
| :--- | :--- |
| **GenAI** | Content generation, summarization, and initial data extraction. |
| **Agentic AI** | Task execution, decision-making, and autonomous workflow management. |
| **RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation)** | Grounding AI responses in trusted, proprietary banking data. |
| **SLMs (Small Language Models)** | Performing highly specialized, secure, and efficient niche tasks (e.g., credit analysis). |
| **Agentic Contracts** | Turning legal text into programmable, self-executing logic. |
