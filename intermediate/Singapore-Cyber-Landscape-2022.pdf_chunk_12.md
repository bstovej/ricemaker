Based on the provided document from the **Singapore Cyber Landscape 2022**, here are the key insights and facts categorized by theme:

### 1. National Cyber Defence & Critical Infrastructure
*   **CIDeX 2022:** The inaugural Critical Infrastructure Defence Exercise (CIDeX) was held in November 2022. It was a large-scale "nation under attack" simulation designed to train operators of Critical Information Infrastructure (CII).
*   **Inter-agency Collaboration:** The exercise was a joint effort between the **Digital and Intelligence Service (DIS)** of the SAF, the **Cyber Security Agency (CSA)**, and academic institutions (NUS and SUTD).
*   **Scope of Protection:** The exercise involved 16 organizations across vital sectors, including **Water, Power, Telecommunications, and Transport** (e.g., SingTel, SMRT, Sembcorp, and ST Engineering).
*   **Focus on OT:** The simulation included not just IT infrastructure, but also **Operational Technology (OT)** physical testbeds, such as simulated power and water plants equipped with sensors.

### 2. Vulnerability Management & Community Engagement
*   **CVDP Success:** Since the launch of the Cyber Vulnerability Disclosure Programme (CVDP) in 2018, over **2,000 members of the public and researchers** have identified and helped remediate more than **1,000 valid vulnerabilities** in government systems.
*   **Incentivizing Researchers:** GovTech rewards researchers through cash bounties for severe vulnerabilities and digital badges for others. This "white hat" community is viewed as a critical component of a "multiplier effect" in identifying system weaknesses.

### 3. Data Protection & SME Security
*   **Mandatory Notifications:** Under the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), organizations are now legally obligated to notify the PDPC and affected individuals of significant data breaches.
*   **Rising Cyber Threats:** In 2022, the PDPC investigated over 60 data breaches; **over 60% of these were categorized as cyber incidents**, primarily driven by ransomware and phishing.
*   **Supporting SMEs (DPE):** The **Data Protection Essentials (DPE)** program was developed to help Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) establish foundational security and data protection capabilities. An example provided is the Georges Group, which implemented DPE to secure customer data.

### 4. Anti-Scam Efforts & Statistics
*   **Escalating Scam Trends (2022):**
    *   **Total Scam Cases:** 31,728 (a **33% increase** from 2021).
    *   **Cybercrime Cases:** 33,669 (a **25% increase**).
    *   **Financial Loss:** Over **S$660.7 million** was lost to scams in 2022.
*   **Recovery Efforts:** The Anti-Scam Command (ASCom) successfully recovered approximately **S$146.6 million** (roughly 27% of the total lost).
*   **ASCom Operations:** Operationalized on March 22, 2022, the Anti-Scam Command (ASCom) integrates various units to provide a unified response. In 2022, it coordinated 25 operations, resulting in the arrest or investigation of over **8,000 scammers and money mules**.
*   **International Cooperation:** The Singapore Police Force (SPF) works with international agencies like the **Royal Malaysia Police** and **Interpol**. In 2022, these collaborations helped take down 13 scam syndicates (including job, impersonation, and phishing syndicates).
*   **The S.C.A.M.S Strategy:** A framework used by ASCom involving: **S**trengthened sense-making, **C**lose collaboration, **A**gile response, **M**aximize effective management, **S**wift and strategic actions.
