A significant portion of the text is dedicated to the difficulty of ensuring that an AI's goals remain aligned with human values (the "Alignment Problem").
*   **The Mind Projection Fallacy:** The author warns against the mistake of assuming that because an AI is "intelligent," it will "think" like a human or possess human-like emotions/desires.
*   **Control Strategies and Their Failures:**
