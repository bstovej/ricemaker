This document, a report from **Citi GPS (January 2025)**, explores the transformative impact of **Agentic AI** on the global workforce, with a specific focus on the financial services sector.

The following are the key insights and facts categorized by thematic area:

### 1. Impact on the Workforce and Talent Pipeline
*   **Augmentation vs. Replacement:** In the short term, Agentic AI is expected to "supercharge" employees rather than directly replace jobs. However, increased productivity (more output with fewer people) is expected to lead to a reduction in overall headcount.
*   **The "Junior Role" Dilemma:** A significant social concern is the potential erosion of entry-level positions (junior engineers, analysts, etc.). If AI automates the tasks traditionally used to train newcomers, the future talent pipeline may be compromised.
*   **Shift in Value:** As technical tasks become automated, "human" skills—specifically **creativity, empathy, and judgment**—are expected to increase in demand and command a premium. There may also be a resurgence in the value of person-to-person contact.
*   **New Role Emergence:** New job categories are emerging, such as AI engineers/trainers, AI data curators, AI ethics & safety officers, and AI UX designers.

### 2. Financial Services: Adoption and Use Cases
*   **Leading Adopters:** Financial services is identified as a leader in AI adoption, specifically measured by Microsoft Azure consumption revenue.
*   **Key Use Cases:**
    *   **Wealth Management/Retail Banking:** Personalized financial advice and customized savings goals.
    *   **Corporate Banking:** Optimized loan structures and dynamic pricing.
    *   **Compliance (KYC/AML):** Using AI to manage the massive volume of sanctions alerts (noting that the industry standard for "false positives" is currently a staggering 95%).
    *   **Risk & Underwriting:** Real-time risk profiling and predictive models for default probability.
    *   **Fraud Prevention:** Detecting unauthorized activity and anomalies in trade finance.
*   **The "Compose" Strategy:** Implementation is moving away from a simple "build vs. buy" decision toward a "compose" model, where firms use modular building blocks and low-code/no-code platforms (e.g., Copilot Studio) to create tailored agents.

### 3. Risk, Governance, and Cybersecurity
*   **Governance Imperatives:** Regulators will focus on how autonomous agents are overseen. Critical areas for firms include:
    *   **Accountability:** Establishing who is responsible when an agent errs and ensuring a "human override" mechanism exists.
    *   **Monitoring:** Continuous oversight to prevent harm to clients and markets.
    *   **Data Privacy:** Ensuring agents handle enterprise data responsibly without unauthorized sharing.
*   **Cybersecurity Threats:** Agentic AI increases the "attack surface" because agents integrate across multiple systems. There is a risk that "army-scale" hacker agents could penetrate firewalls at a very low cost, making traditional deterministic defenses obsolete.
*   **The "Human-in-the-Loop" Necessity:** A human oversight function remains critical to detect violations and ensure agents function according to policy.

### 4. Regulatory and Ethical Landscape
*   **The Right to Contest:** The European Commission (EC) emphasizes that customers should not be subject to legally binding decisions based *solely* on automated processing; they must have the right to contest outcomes.
*   **Regulatory Sandboxes:** Following Spain’s lead, more "regulatory sandboxes" are expected to emerge to test Agentic AI applications under controlled conditions.
*   **Ethical Uncertainty:** The lack of a defined "moral compass" in AI remains a barrier to large-scale adoption in banking, as firms are held liable for AI-driven errors.

### 5. Emerging Threats: Deepfakes and Fraud
*   **The Deepfake Explosion:** Deepfake scams have increased by over **2,000%** in the last three years.
*   **Economic Impact:** The Global Anti-Scam Alliance estimated that **$1 trillion** was lost to scams in 2023.
*   **Technological Sophistication:** Generative AI has reached a point where synthetic audio and video are nearly indistinguishable from reality, posing a massive threat to biometric security and identity verification in finance.
