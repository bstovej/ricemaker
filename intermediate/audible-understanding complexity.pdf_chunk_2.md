### 5. Key Strategic Concepts
*   **The "Edge of Chaos" Strategy:** In business or biological evolution, the most "fit" entities are those that exist on the boundary between extreme stability (stagnation) and extreme instability (chaos).
*   **Exploration vs. Exploitation:** A fundamental tension in complex systems. "Exploration" (searching for new possibilities/mutation) is necessary for long-term survival, but "Exploitation" (using known resources/stability) is necessary for short-term efficiency.
*   **Robustness vs. Fragility:** High complexity can lead to **Robustness** (the ability to withstand shocks) but can also create **Fragility** (where a small failure in one part causes a systemic collapse, often called "cascading failure").
