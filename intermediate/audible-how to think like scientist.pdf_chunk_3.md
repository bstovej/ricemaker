### 5. Key Philosophical and Psychological Concepts
*   **The "Blind Spot" of Bias:** The text notes that we are often aware of biases in others but struggle to recognize them in ourselves (the "bias blind spot").
*   **The Power of Framing:** How a question or situation is "framed" can drastically change the decision or response.
*   **Cognitive Dissonance:** The mental discomfort experienced when holding two conflicting beliefs, leading to a drive to resolve the inconsistency.

### Summary of Core Lessons
