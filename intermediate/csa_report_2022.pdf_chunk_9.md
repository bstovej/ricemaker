Based on the document segments provided from the **Singapore Cyber Landscape 2022** report, here are the key insights and facts categorized by theme:

### 1. International Leadership & Diplomacy
*   **UN Leadership:** Singapore plays a prominent role in the United Nations, specifically **chairing the ongoing five-year UN Open-ended Working Group (OEWG) on the Security of and in the use of ICTs (2021–2025).**
*   **Unique Position in Ransomware Defense:** Singapore holds a unique status as the **only ASEAN Member State (AMS) currently invited to be a member of the Counter-Ransomware Initiative (CRI)**, a global group of 39 countries plus the EU.
*   **Bilateral Strengthenings:** 
    *   **US-Singapore Relations:** The **US-Singapore Cyber Dialogue (USCD)** was established in March 2022 to discuss critical infrastructure, supply chain security, and digital scams. A specific working group was also formed regarding the "Intersection of Technology and Cyber."
    *   **Germany:** Singapore and Germany signed a **Mutual Recognition Arrangement** to recognize cybersecurity labels issued by their respective agencies.
    *   **China:** Singapore engaged in bilateral discussions with China regarding digital cooperation under the ambit of China’s Dialogue Partnership with ASEAN.

### 2. Major Cybersecurity Initiatives & Alliances
*   **ASEAN CERT Incident Drill (ACID) 2022:** The 17th ACID focused on "Dealing with Disruptive Cyber-Attacks Arising from Exploitation of Vulnerabilities," specifically responding to the impact of the **Log4j vulnerabilities**. It involved 15 CERTs from ASEAN and Dialogue Partners.
*   **Points of Contact (POCs) Directory:** As part of the UN Confidence-builders Group, Singapore is working on a proposal to establish a **global, inter-governmental points of contact (POCs) directory** to enhance international communication during cyber incidents.
*   **Counter-Ransomware Cooperation:** In 2022, Singapore and the UK co-led a working group specifically aimed at **countering the illicit financing activities** used by ransomware actors.

### 3. Capacity Building & Training
*   **UN-Singapore Cyber Fellowship (UNSCF):** A flagship joint initiative between Singapore and the UN Office of Disarmament Affairs (UNODA). 
    *   **Goal:** To empower senior officials (Cyber Ambassadors/heads of national agencies) with inter-disciplinary expertise in policy, strategy, and operations.
    *   **Inaugural Session:** Held in August 2022, featuring **22 Fellows from 18 different countries** across Asia, Africa, Europe, and South America.

### 4. Key Industry Events & Conferences
*   **7th Singapore International Cyber Week (SICW):** Held in-person from October 18–20, 2022 (after two years of virtual formats). It brought together over **10,000 stakeholders from 65 countries** to discuss cybersecurity issues.
*   **Global Engagements:** Singaporean cybersecurity leadership (notably from the CSA) was active in major forums, including the **Tallinn Digital Summit (TDS)** in Estonia and the **Aspen Global Cybersecurity Group** in the US.
