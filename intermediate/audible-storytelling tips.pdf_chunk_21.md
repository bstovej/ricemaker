The text identifies two primary modes of narrative delivery:
*   **Summary (Telling):** Used for background, transitions, and covering long periods of time (e.g., "He lived there for twenty years"). This accelerates the pace.
*   **Scene (Showing):** Used for dramatic, high-stakes moments. This slows the pace and immerses the reader in the immediate experience.
