### 2. The Crisis in Academia
The document highlights a fundamental struggle for universities to remain relevant in the "Big AI" era:
*   **The Resource Gap:** Universities struggle to compete with the private sector due to the massive costs of compute and the ability of "Big Tech" to poach talent (e.g., the mention of Carnegie Mellon losing 40% of researchers to Uber).
*   **Research Objectives:** A core tension exists between the **private sector** (driven by profit, short-term goals, and closed/proprietary results) and **academia** (driven by societal benefit, long-term perspectives, and the need for reproducible, transparent research).
*   **The "CERN for AI" Vision:** To counter private sector dominance, there are calls for large-scale, public-funded initiatives, such as a European "CERN for AI," to provide an attractive research environment for the public interest.
