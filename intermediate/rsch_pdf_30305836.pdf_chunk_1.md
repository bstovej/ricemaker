This document, a **Citi GPS report from January 2025**, explores the transition from Generative AI (GenAI) to **Agentic AI** and its potential to revolutionize the financial services industry through the emergence of a **"Do It For Me" (DIFM) economy.**

Below are the key insights and facts categorized by theme:

### 1. Defining the Paradigm Shift: From GenAI to Agentic AI
The report identifies a fundamental evolution in artificial intelligence:
*   **Generative AI (GenAI):** Focuses on content creation (text, images, code) based on user prompts. It represents a "Show It to Me" economy.
*   **Agentic AI:** Goes beyond content to **autonomous action.** These systems can make decisions, create plans, and execute tasks without human intervention or constant prompting. It represents a "Do It For Me" economy.
*   **The "Digital Jarvis" Concept:** The report envisions users having personal AI agents (similar to Iron Man’s Jarvis) that act as "super competent colleagues" or private bankers in a smartphone, capable of managing complex workflows.

### 2. Economic & Structural Implications
The rise of Agentic AI is expected to trigger a massive economic shift:
*   **The "AI Flywheel":** Progress is accelerating through a self-reinforcing loop where advanced AI leads to better computing systems, which in turn enables even more advanced AI.
*   **Startup Economics:** Agentic AI may usher in a "golden age" for startups by shifting the cost of scaling from **employee payroll (human labor) to software subscriptions (AI agents).**
*   **Impact Magnitude:** The report suggests that Agentic AI could have a more significant impact on the global economy and finance than the internet era.
*   **The Future of Work:** Tasks currently outsourced to third-party contractors or overseas may increasingly be handled by autonomous agents. The role of human workers may shift from managing people to managing fleets of digital agents.

### 3. High-Impact Use Cases in Finance
The report outlines how Agentic AI will be applied across various financial sectors:
*   **Wealth Management & Retail Banking:** Adaptive financial advice, real-time savings optimization, and virtual financial assistants.
*   **Corporate Banking:** Custom lending structures, automated invoice processing, and real-time AML (Anti-Money Laundering) compliance.
*   **Institutional Investing:** Dynamic investment portfolios, real-time market alerts, and automated fund review reports.
*   **Insurance:** Tailored policies, adaptive claim management, and automated premium calculations.
*   **Risk & Operations:** Real-time risk profiling, fraud prevention through contextual analysis, and automated KYC (Know Your Customer) onboarding workflows.

### 4. Critical Trends and Risks
*   **Explosive Growth in Interest:** References to "Agentic AI" in BigTech corporate documents and press articles increased **17x in 2024**, with projections of "parabolic" growth in 2025.
*   **The "Bad Bot" Threat:** The report notes that bots already account for roughly **50% of all global internet traffic**, much of which is malicious. The rise of Agentic AI increases the scale of potential cyber threats, such as deepfakes and automated fraud.
*   **Investment Landscape:** In 2024, **37% of all VC funding** went to AI startups, with "autonomous agents and digital co-workers" seeing the highest growth in deal activity.
*   **Regulatory Necessity:** As agents gain autonomy, the need for robust governance, cybersecurity, and "Agentic Contracts" becomes critical to prevent unintended consequences.
