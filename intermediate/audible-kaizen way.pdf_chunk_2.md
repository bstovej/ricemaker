### 1. The Core Kaizen Framework
The document outlines a consistent three-step methodology for approaching any life goal (Health, Relationships, or Career):

    *   **Focus:** Portion control, exercise, quitting bad habits, and healthy eating.

### 3. The Principle of "Small Rewards"
The document emphasizes that rewards are essential to sustain a Kaizen plan, but they must follow three specific criteria:
1.  **Appropriate to the Goal:** The reward must not contradict the objective (e.g., do not use food as a reward if the goal is weight loss).
2.  **Appropriate to the Person:** The reward should be something the individual enjoys and that does not annoy others.
3.  **Low Cost/No Cost:** Rewards should be inexpensive or free (e.g., a short walk, a self-compliment, a warm bath, or listening to music).
