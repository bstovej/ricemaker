This document is a Goldman Sachs Global Investment Research report (Issue 146) focusing on the impact of Artificial Intelligence (AI) across various global sectors. The overarching theme is the **bifurcation of industries**: some are highly vulnerable to AI-driven "disintermediation," while others are uniquely positioned to leverage AI for efficiency and growth.

Below are the key insights and facts organized by sector:

### 1. Advertising and Consumer Internet
*   **The "Automation" Trend:** AI-powered tools (like Google’s Performance Max and Meta’s Advantage+) are successfully integrating multiple use cases, automating ad services, and improving spend efficiency.
*   **Economic Impact Projections:** The report identifies four massive shifts driven by AI:
    *   **$170bn:** Shift of ad spend from traditional to digital channels.
    *   **$114bn:** Potential for automated ad creative generation.
    *   **$25bn:** Consolidation of ad tech intermediaries.
    *   **$161bn:** Disruption of traditional ad agencies.
*   **Strategic Responses:** Internet platforms are using a mix of **"offense"** (launching new AI consumer apps) and **"defense"** (upgrading existing apps to prevent disruption).
*   **Investment Opportunity:** The analysts maintain a **"Buy"** rating on **Spotify (SPOT)** and **DoorDash (DAS H)**, noting that the market is currently undervaluing their resilience and data advantages.

### 2. US Business and Information Services
*   **Valuation Compression:** The sector has seen a significant "de-rating" due to AI fears, with the median NTM P/E multiple dropping from **32x (June 2025) to 20x (current)**.
*   **The "Moat" Divide:**
    *   **Winners (Resilient):** Companies with proprietary data, regulatory moats, or entrenched workflows (e.g., **FICO, MCO, MSCI, SPGI, TRI, VRSK**). These firms are positioned to benefit from increased pricing power and cross-selling.
    *   **Losers (Vulnerable):** Companies with limited proprietary content or those providing "rote" services at risk of automation (e.g., **FDS, HRB, RHI**).

### 3. North American Insurance
*   **The ChatGPT Threat:** The integration of insurance services into ChatGPT has sparked fears of disruption, particularly for **personal lines and micro-business brokers** (auto/home insurance).
*   **Resilience in Complexity:** **Commercial (re)insurers** and **large brokers** are well-positioned because their business models rely on complex consulting, regulated capital allocation, and deep structural moats.
*   **Efficiency Gains:** Large-cap firms (e.g., **AIG, CB, AON**) are expected to benefit first from AI automation in claims processing and policy handling.

### 4. Commercial Real Estate (CRE) Services
*   **Overstated Risk:** The report argues that AI disruption fears for CRE (e.g., **CBRE, JLL**) are significantly overstated because the industry is fundamentally **relationship-driven and physical**.
*   **Long-term Adoption:** Unlike software, CRE technology adoption unfolds over decades. AI is expected to enhance functions (scheduling, maintenance) rather than replace brokers.
*   **Market Opportunity:** The sector is currently trading at a discount due to transaction volume lows, presenting an attractive entry point.

### 5. European Financials and Logistics
*   **LSEG Resilience:** Despite a 25% share price decline, **LSEG** is viewed as resilient because 70% of its Workflow segment revenue is tied to regulated trading and indices, which are difficult for AI to replicate.
*   **Private Markets:** Private equity is benefiting from AI-driven digital infrastructure demand, though private credit managers face higher risks due to their sensitivity to market dispersion.
*   **Logistics Efficiency:** In the transport sector, "asset-light" companies like **DSV** are prime candidates for AI-driven margin expansion through the automation of repetitive tasks (quoting, booking, customs).

### Summary of Key Economic Indicators (Glossary)
The document also defines several proprietary GS indices used for tracking economic health:
*   **CAI (Current Activity Indicator):** A real-time alternative to the lagging GDP.
*   **DEER:** A framework for determining "fair" exchange rate values.
*   **FCI (Financial Conditions Index):** Measures the "looseness" or "tightness" of global economies.
*   **MAP (Macro-Data Assessment Platform):** A tool to quickly interpret the importance and "surprise" factor of new economic data.
