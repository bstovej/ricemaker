### **4. Summary of Statistical Methods Used**
*   **Analysis of Variance (ANOVA):** Used to partition total variability into components attributable to specific factors and error.
*   **Factorial Design:** Used to study the effects of multiple factors and their interactions simultaneously.
*   **F-Tests:** Used to determine the statistical significance of main effects and interaction effects.
*   **Residual Analysis:** Used to verify the assumptions of normality, constant variance, and independence of the error terms.
