This document, part of a **Citi GPS (Global Perspectives & Solutions)** report from January 2025, explores the transformative potential of **Agentic AI** within the financial services industry, specifically focusing on investment research and portfolio management.

Below are the key insights and facts extracted from the text.

### 1. Strategic Value Proposition
*   **Efficiency over Scale:** The primary value of Agentic AI is its ability to extract more utility from *existing* models without requiring more data. It shifts the focus from model size to making models more "actionable and application-oriented."
*   **The "Agent Associate" Model:** The document proposes a structural shift in the finance workforce, where a senior analyst functions as a manager overseeing a "group of agent associates" to handle high-volume, repetitive tasks.
*   **Speed to Market:** Agentic AI can reduce the time required to respond to breaking news from hours to minutes, allowing analysts to interact with clients almost immediately after market events occur.

### 2. Key Use Cases in Finance
The document categorizes the application of Agentic AI into three main areas:

**A. Investment Research & Due Diligence**
*   **Data Ingestion:** Automating the searching, extracting, and summarizing of news, research reports, and financial statements into spreadsheets.
*   **Funnel Management:** Using AI to narrow down large lists of investment targets by constructing complex queries and analyzing data against specific criteria.
*   **Document Generation:** Automatically populating investment proposals and creating market research reports, press releases, and thought leadership pieces.
*   **Style Preservation:** The ability to generate content that maintains a specific company brand, accuracy, and an individual analyst’s unique writing style.

**B. Portfolio Construction & Management**
*   **Rebalancing:** Providing investment ideas and suggestions for rebalancing portfolios based on real-time market information.
*   **Simulation:** Running "what-if" scenarios by plugging different assumptions into models and spreadsheets to predict outcomes.

**C. Client Relationship Management**
*   **Hyper-Personalization:** Analyzing unstructured data (such as recordings of client calls) to tailor portfolios to specific client preferences.
*   **Automated Reporting:** Summarizing client portfolios to keep managers updated and generating customized client reports.

### 3. Technical Framework: How Agentic AI Works
The document moves beyond "simple bots" to describe a sophisticated technical architecture:

*   **The Augmented LLM:** The foundation is not just a standard Large Language Model, but an "Augmented LLM" enhanced with three critical components:
    1.  **Retrieval:** Accessing external information.
    2.  **Tools:** The ability to execute specific tasks or use software.
    3 de**Memory:** Providing contextual continuity for personalized interactions.
*   **Orchestrator-Worker Workflow:** A complex task-management structure where a central "Orchestrator" LLM breaks down a complex problem, delegates specific sub-tasks to "Worker" LLMs, and then synthesizes the results into a final output.
*   **Autonomous Agents:** These are systems that operate in a loop, using tools and reacting to environmental feedback to achieve goals within predefined parameters.

### 4. Evolutionary Context of "Agents"
The text provides a historical perspective on the evolution of agency in finance:
*   **Phase 1 (Human):** Human intermediaries (brokers, advisers) acting for a principal.
*   **Phase 2 (Automated):** Electronic trading and algorithmic bots operating on simple, rule-based logic.
*   **Phase 3 (Agentic):** The current generation of AI that can **reason, learn, and make decisions** autonomously.
