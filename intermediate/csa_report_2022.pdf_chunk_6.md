### **1. Core Strategic Framework**
The document outlines the **Singapore Cybersecurity Strategy 2021**, which is built upon three strategic pillars and two foundational enablers:

*   **Strategic Pillar 1: Build Resilient Infrastructure** (Strengthening digital infrastructure).
*   **Strategic Pillar 2: Enable a Safer Cyberspace** (Creating a cleaner/healthier digital environment).
*   **Strategic Pillar 3: Enhance International Cyber Cooperation** (Promoting international norms and standards).
*   **Foundational Enabler 1:** Develop a Vibrant Cybersecurity Ecosystem.
*   **Foundational Enabler 2:** Grow a Robust Cyber Talent Pipeline.

---

### **2. Key Initiatives: Pillar 1 (Resilient Infrastructure)**
The focus in 2022 was on strengthening Critical Information Infrastructure (CII) through regulation, guidelines, and proactive testing.

*   **CII Supply Chain Programme:** Launched in July 2022, this is a national effort to manage the complex risks of the cyber supply chain. It consists of five key initiatives:
    1.  Cyber Supply Chain Assessment Toolkit.
    2.  Cyber Contractual Handbook for CIIs.
    3.  Vendor Certification Programme.
    4.  Cyber Supply Chain Learning Hub.
    5.  A platform for International Cooperation.
*   **Cybersecurity Code of Practice (CCoP) 2nd Edition:** Issued on July 4, 2022, to help CII owners adopt a threat-based approach and remain agile against emerging risks (such as 5G technologies).
*   **Legislation & Regulation:** The *Cybersecurity (Composition of Offences) Regulations 2022* (effective April 8, 2022) allows for "compoundable offences," meaning fines can be paid in lieu of prosecution to optimize court resources.
*   **Proactive Monitoring & Exercises:**
    *   **Advisories:** The National Cyber Threat Monitoring Centre (NCTMC) issued **102 advisories** in 2022, including a significant alert regarding the **"Pipedream" malware toolkit**.
    *   **Exercises:** Five CII sectors completed sectoral cyber exercises in 2022. Notably, **Exercise Cyber Star 2021** involved over 200 participants testing responses to ransomware and OT (Operational Technology) system compromises.

### **3. Key Initiatives: Pillar 2 (Safer Cyberspace)**
The focus was on public awareness and community resilience, specifically targeting younger and older demographics.

*   **SG Cyber Safe Students Programme:** Launched July 13, 2022, to educate students on online dangers.
    *   **Social Media Engagement:** The "Better Cyber Safe Than Sorry" music video achieved significant reach (44,000+ views on Instagram; 10,000+ on YouTube).
    *   **Community Involvement:** The `#BetterCyberSafeSG` school dance challenge engaged over 300 students across seven schools.
*   **Intergenerational Learning:** A program where students were paired with seniors to provide one-on-one tutorials on using digital apps safely.
*   **Scam Prevention:** In partnership with the Singapore Police Force, the "Go Safe Online Pop-up" provided updated handbooks on current scam trends.
