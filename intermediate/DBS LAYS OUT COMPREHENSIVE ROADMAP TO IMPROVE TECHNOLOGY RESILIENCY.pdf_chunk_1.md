This document is a news release from **DBS Group Holdings Ltd**, dated November 1, 2023. It serves as a formal apology for a series of digital banking disruptions experienced throughout 2023 and outlines a strategic "Technology Resiliency Roadmap" to prevent future occurrences.

### **1. Core Context & Cause**
*   **The Issue:** DBS experienced multiple digital service disruptions in 2023, specifically citing incidents on March 29, May 5, September 26 (impacted FAST/PayNow), October 14 (data centre incident), and October 20 (DBS PayLah! access).
*   **The Investigation:** An independent review by **Accenture** identified four primary areas of deficiency:
    1.  Technology risk governance and oversight.
  	2.  Incident management.
  	3.  System resilience.
  	4.  Change management.
*   **Accountability:** DBS Chairman Peter Seah stated that senior management will be held accountable, with this reflected in their compensation.

### **2. The Technology Resiliency Roadmap (Key Actions)**
The bank is implementing a mix of structural, operational, and leadership changes:

**A. Governance & Oversight**
*   **New Committee:** Established the **BRMC Technology Risk Committee (BTRC)** to provide dedicated oversight of technology risk.
*   **Structural Realignment:** Transferred the Technology Risk Management team to the Risk Management Group (reporting to the Chief Risk Officer) to ensure better "checks and balances."

**B. Organizational & Leadership Changes**
*   **Departmental Split:** Effective November 1, 2023, the **Technology & Operations (T&O)** function is being split into two separate units to allow for dedicated management.
*   **Key Appointments:** 
    *   **Jimmy Ng** moves from CIO/Head of T&O to **Group Head of Operations**.
    *   **Han Kwee Juan** assumes the role of **Acting CIO** while a search for a permanent CIO is underway.
    *   **Sim S. Lim** returns temporarily to manage day-to-day Singapore operations.
*   **New Quality Assurance:** Created a new Quality Assurance function within the Enterprise Architecture Site Reliability Engineering (EASRE) to oversee the change management process.

**C. Operational Measures**
*   **IT Freeze:** The bank has instituted a **six-month pause on all non-essential IT activities** to focus exclusively on resiliency.
*   **Enhanced Management:** Implementing clearer ownership of incidents and improving proactive problem management through "early warning indicators."

### **3. Financial Commitment & Service Targets**
*   **Budget:** DBS has allocated a special budget of **SGD 80 million** specifically to enhance system resiliency.
*   **New Service Availability Targets:** DBS is moving beyond regulatory requirements to set its own targets for three key services: **Balance Enquiry, Overseas Payments, and Domestic Payments.**
    *   **Downtime Target:** Aiming to limit total downtime (where the service is unavailable across all channels) to an average of **no more than 1.5 hours per month** (measured over a three-month period).
*   **Service Recovery Targets:**
    *   **Short-term (6 months):** Aiming to recover services within **3 hours** of a disruption.
    *   **Long-term (24 months):** Aiming to reduce recovery time to **2 hours or less**.
