Based on the provided document segment from the **Singapore Cyber Landscape 2022**, here are the key insights and facts organized by thematic area:

### 1. Strategic Goal: Building a Cybersecurity Ecosystem
Singapore is focusing on a "Foundational Enabler" approach to create a vibrant, research-driven ecosystem.
*   **National Cybersecurity R&D (NCR) Programme:** Aims to drive innovation for security, reliability, and usability through collaboration between government agencies (including CSA, NRF, and GovTech) and academia.
*   **CyberCall (CSA Industry Call for Innovation):** A platform designed to catalyze the development of innovative solutions (e.g., AI, IoT, Cloud security, and OT security) by connecting companies with trusted end-users in Singapore.
*   **Success Story (Scantist):** The document highlights **Scantist**, a spin-off from Nanyang Technological University (NTU), as a primary beneficiary of the NCR Programme. Scantist successfully transitioned from a university lab to a commercial entity, specializing in software vulnerability identification.

### 2. Strategic Goal: Developing a Robust Talent Pipeline
A major priority is growing a workforce that spans from youth to senior leadership.
*   **SG Cyber Olympians:** A programme that identifies and trains talented Singaporean youth for international competitions, such as the **Cyber SEA Games**. In 2022, 47 youths were identified, and 7 top performers were selected to represent Singapore.
*   **Cybersecurity Strategic Leadership Programme (CSLP):** Developed in partnership with **Singapore Management University (SMU)**, this is an executive MBA-style programme for senior leaders in Critical Information Infrastructure (CII) sectors (e.g., Energy, Finance, Healthcare). The inaugural cohort included 21 leaders.
*   **Smart Nation Scholarship:** A joint initiative by **CSA, GovTech, and IMDA** to recruit talent into seven key tech domains, including Cybersecurity, Data Science & AI, and Tech Policy & Governance. 17 scholarships were awarded in 2022.
*   **Exercise Cyber Knights 2021 (XCK21):** A specialized exercise conducted to sharpen the skills of Singapore’s cyber responders.

### 3. International Collaboration and Diplomacy
Singapore is actively engaging in regional and global cybersecurity norms and capacity building.
*   **ASEAN-Singapore Cybersecurity Centre of Excellence (ASCCE):** Through the ASCCE Webinar Series, Singapore maintains momentum in capacity building for ASEAN Member States (AMS), covering topics like Cyber Stability Frameworks and Cybersecurity Diplomacy.
*   **Global Norms and UN Collaboration:** Singapore participates in workshops such as the **Norms Implementation Checklist Workshop**, a joint initiative with **UNODA** (United Nations Office for Disarmament Affairs), focusing on the responsible behavior of states in cyberspace.
*   **Broadened Scope of Cybersecurity:** The document emphasizes that cybersecurity is moving beyond "technical-only" solutions to include non-technical pillars: strategy, legislation, diplomacy, and crisis communication.

### 4. Key Agencies and Partners
The document identifies a highly integrated ecosystem of government and academic partners:
*   **Lead Agencies:** Cyber Security Agency of Singapore (**CSA**), Infocomm Media Development Authority (**IMDA**), and **GovTech**.
*   **Key Partners:** National Research Foundation (NRF), UNODA, SMU, and various ASEAN Dialogue Partners.

### Summary of Core Themes
*   **Integration:** Moving from purely technical/hardware solutions to include policy, law, and diplomacy.
*   **Sustainability:** Using seed funding and grants (like CyberCall) to turn academic research into commercialized, job-creating enterprises.
*   **Resilience:** Strengthening both the "human" element (students and leaders) and the "technical" element (R&D and infrastructure protection).
