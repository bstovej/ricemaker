The document makes a sharp distinction between "fortune-telling" and "foresight."

*   **The Utility of Scenarios:** The text highlights that the Singapore government uses **scenario planning** as a whole-of-government effort. The goal is not to be "right" about the future, but to identify assumptions, biases, and blind spots to enable better decision-making.
*   **Acting on "Weak Signals":** Effective foresight involves detecting "weak signals" and acting on them before they turn into "thick entanglements" (unmanageable crises).
