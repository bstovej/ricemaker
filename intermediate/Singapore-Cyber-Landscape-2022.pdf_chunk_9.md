This is evidenced by Singapore **chairing** the ongoing five-year UN Open-ended Working Group (OEWG) and being the **only ASEAN Member State (AMS) invited** to join the Counter-Ransomware Initiative (CRI).

### **Key Facts**

#### **1. Major Events & Initiatives**
*   **7th Singapore International Cyber Week (SICW):** Held in-person from October 18–20, 2022; it brought together over 10,000 stakeholders from 65 countries.
*   **Counter-Ransomware Initiative (CRI):** A group of 39 countries plus the EU. Singapore is the sole ASEAN member. Singapore and the UK co-led a working group specifically targeting the illicit financing of ransomware.
*   **17th ASEAN CERT Incident Drill (ACID) 2022:** Focused on "triple extortion" ransomware tactics (inspired by the Log4j vulnerabilities). It involved 15 participating CERTs from ASEAN and Dialogue Partners.

#### **2. International Governance & Diplomacy**
*   **UN OEWG (2021–2025):** Singapore holds the Chairmanship of this ongoing five-year group regarding the security of and use of ICTs.
*   **US-Singapore Cyber Dialogue (USSCD):** Established in March 2022 to discuss critical infrastructure, supply chain security, and digital scams.
*   **ASEAN-China Cooperation:** Significant engagement occurred in 2022, including bilateral meetings to discuss digital cooperation under the framework of China’s Dialogue Partnership with ASEAN.

#### **3. Bilateral Agreements & Cooperation**
*   **Germany:** Singapore and Germany established a **Mutual Recognition Arrangement** to recognize cybersecurity labels issued by their respective agencies (CSA and BSI).
*   **US Engagement:** High-level visits included the US National Cyber Director, Chris Inglis, who met with Singapore’s Senior Minister Teo Chee Hean.

#### **4. Educational & Capacity Building**
*   **UN-Singapore Cyber Fellowship (UNSCF):** A joint initiative with the UN Office of Disarmament Affairs (UNODA). The inaugural session in August 2022 included 22 Fellows from 18 countries across Asia, Africa, Europe, and South America.
