### **Key Facts**

#### **Quantum Computing & Market Trends**
*   **Market Growth:** The quantum computing market was worth **US$412 million in 2020** and is projected to reach **US$8.6 billion by 2027** (per IDC). Long-term projections suggest it could exceed **US$90 billion by 2040**.
*   **Hardware Milestones:** 
    *   IBM unveiled the **433-qubit Osprey** processor in November 2022.
    *   IBM aims to deliver a **1,121-qubit processor** by the end of 2023.
    *   IBM aims to surpass **4,000 qubits by 2025**.
*   **Technical Distinction:** Unlike classical computers that use binary "bits" (0 or 1), quantum computers use **"qubits,"** which can exist in a **superposition** (representing both 0 and 1 simultaneously), enabling exponential increases in processing power.

#### **Global Investment**
*   **United Kingdom:** Announced **£2.5 billion** in state funding for quantum research in March 2023.
*   **China:** Reportedly increased state funding to **US$15 billion**.

#### **Cybersecurity Definitions (from Glossary)**
*   **Advanced Persistent Threat (APT):** Long-term, undetected attacks often conducted by state-sponsored actors to exfiltrate or destroy data.
*   **Zero Day Vulnerability:** A vulnerability in a system that has been disclosed but has not yet been patched.
*   **Ransomware:** Malware that encrypts a victim's files, demanding payment (usually in cryptocurrency) to restore access.
*   **DDoS (Distributed Denial-of-Service):** An attack that attempts to prevent legitimate users from accessing a service by flooding a network with traffic.
